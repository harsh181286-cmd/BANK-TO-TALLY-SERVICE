This information is used only to prepare your Tally-compatible XML file and to communicate with you about your order on WhatsApp. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

Your bank statement and related details are used only for the time needed to prepare your file and are not stored, sold, or shared with any third party beyond that.

1 bank statement = up to 5 pages. Statements longer than 5 pages will be counted as multiple statements against your plan quota. The Quick plan covers 1 statement, the Smart plan covers 15 statements, and the Premium plan covers up to 150 statements valid for 1 year from activation.

Turnaround time

Turnaround time begins once both your payment and your bank statement have been received on WhatsApp.

Payments

All payments are final and non-refundable once made.

Accuracy of details

Your Tally file is prepared strictly based on the company name, ledger names, and narration mappings you provide. Please verify these details before sending, as we are not responsible for errors arising from incorrect details supplied by you.

Corrections

If there is an error in the file we prepared due to a mistake on our end, message us and we will correct it at no extra cost.

Data use

We do not store or share your bank statement or ledger details beyond the time needed to prepare your file. See our Privacy Policy for details.
